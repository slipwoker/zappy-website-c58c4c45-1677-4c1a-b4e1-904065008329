Understanding Your Rights

What Are Surplus Funds?

Surplus funds may arise when a property is sold through foreclosure, tax sale, or a similar public auction process, and the final sale price exceeds the total amount owed on the property. As part of our financial recovery services, we help former owners identify and reclaim these funds.

When a property is sold through a tax sale, foreclosure, or similar public process, the remaining balance — after all liens, taxes, fees, and other obligations are satisfied — is generally referred to as surplus funds or overage funds. These amounts are held through county-specific procedures and may be available for review by former owners or related parties.

The availability, amount, and eligibility for surplus funds are determined through public records and county-specific legal requirements. Many former property owners are unaware these funds exist or that they may have a valid claim. Our role is to identify, review, and guide eligible claimants through every step of the recovery process — at no upfront cost.

Example Scenario

Surplus Funds Calculation

If a property has $15,000 owed in back taxes and is sold for $300,000:

Sale Price $300,000
Amount Owed(taxes, liens, fees & obligations) − $15,000
Surplus Funds $285,000

The above is a simplified illustrative example. Actual surplus fund amounts, eligibility, and procedures are determined by public records and applicable county and state regulations.
