Understanding What Client Experiences Tell You

The client experiences shared on this page reflect individual outcomes from specific surplus fund recovery matters. Each case presented a unique set of circumstances, and the results achieved were shaped by the particular facts of that property, county, and legal process.

Surplus fund recovery is not a guaranteed service. The availability of funds, the amount recoverable, and the timeline for resolution vary considerably from case to case. Piedmont Triad Equity Solutions provides professional financial recovery services on a contingency basis — we only receive compensation upon a successful recovery — but we make no representation that any particular outcome will be achieved in your matter.

Past results in surplus fund recovery proceedings are not indicative of future outcomes. You should not rely on any client experience described on this site as a prediction, promise, or guarantee of what may occur in your individual case.

Piedmont Triad Equity Solutions operates with full transparency as part of our commitment to ethical financial recovery services.

Result Variability Notice

Factors that affect every claim

  1. 01

    Property History & Record Completeness

    Each property carries a distinct ownership and lien history. Incomplete or disputed records can affect the identification of eligible claimants and the amount of surplus funds available for recovery.

  2. 02

    County Procedures & Jurisdictional Rules

    Surplus fund procedures differ by county and state. The specific rules governing how funds are held, claimed, and distributed are determined by local statutes and the practices of individual county offices.

  3. 03

    Court Timelines & Scheduling

    Court dockets, hearing schedules, and judicial review periods vary by jurisdiction. These timelines are outside our control and can affect the overall duration of the recovery process in any given case.

  4. 04

    Administrative & Competing Claim Factors

    Competing lienholders, creditor claims, or administrative holds placed by a government entity may reduce the net amount recoverable. Final disbursements are subject to satisfaction of all valid prior obligations.

This notice applies to all client experiences, case summaries, and recovery amounts referenced throughout this website. For questions about your specific situation, contact a dedicated recovery specialist.
