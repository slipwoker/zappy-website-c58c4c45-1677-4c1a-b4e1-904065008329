About Our Firm

Piedmont Triad
Equity Solutions

Piedmont Triad Equity Solutions is a nationwide surplus fund recovery firm headquartered in North Carolina. We specialize in helping former property owners identify and reclaim funds left over after foreclosures, tax sales, and public auctions — money that is rightfully theirs, yet often goes unclaimed.

Our practice is built on a foundation of trust, transparency, and confidentiality. We operate entirely on a contingency basis, meaning there are never any upfront fees — our financial recovery services are compensated only upon a successful recovery. Every client receives a dedicated recovery specialist who guides them through each step of the process, ensuring clarity and peace of mind from first contact to final resolution.

Our Charter of Service

The following guarantees form the foundation of every engagement we undertake. These are not aspirations — they are binding commitments that govern our financial recovery services from the first consultation to successful fund recovery.

  1. I

    No Upfront Fees — Ever

    We operate exclusively on a contingency basis. You pay nothing unless we successfully recover funds on your behalf. There are no retainers, no application fees, and no hidden charges at any stage of the process. Our success is directly tied to yours.

  2. II

    Complete Transparency at Every Stage

    You will receive clear, timely updates throughout your claim. We explain every step, every document, and every timeline in plain language — no legal jargon, no ambiguity. Our financial recovery process is designed to keep you fully informed and in control at all times.

  3. III

    Strict Confidentiality of Your Information

    All personal details, property records, and claim-related information you share with us are handled with the highest standard of discretion. We never share, sell, or disclose your information to third parties outside the direct requirements of your recovery claim.

  4. IV

    A Dedicated Recovery Specialist for Every Claim

    Every client is assigned a single, named recovery specialist who manages your case from intake through resolution. You will always know who to contact, and your specialist will know the full history of your claim — no handoffs, no confusion, no starting over.
